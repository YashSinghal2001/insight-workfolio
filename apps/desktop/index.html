Insight Workfolio

Checking connection…
Transparent monitoring. This app records your clock-in, break and clock-out times. While you are clocked in and not on a break, it may also record which application is in front, when you are idle and, if your organization turns them on and you acknowledge the notice, screenshots of your main screen. Each is shown below with whether it is on right now. It never records keystrokes or window titles, and it reads nothing from your browsers.

Insight Workfolio keeps running in the menu bar while you are signed in, so attendance, activity monitoring and screenshots continue exactly as shown here. Open this window again from the menu bar icon. To stop recording, choose Quit Insight Workfolio from that menu.

Update available

Your organization requires this version. You can finish your current shift and clock out, but clocking in waits until the app is updated.
